Sending rural youth to global coding adventures.

International hackathons, cultural immersion, and real-world teamwork that broaden horizons.

What is Adventures?

A program that brings rural coders to international hackathons and coding projects, bringing a fresh global perspective to the rural community. The program is supported by fundraisers and corporate partners.

Global Exposure

Teens from rural communities are placed in an unfamiliar global setting. It allows them to meet diverse people beyond the scope of their rural environment.

Hands-On Projects

Rural teens work on hands-on projects on these adventures, fostering new computer science skills with teammates all over the world.

Growth

In working with diverse demographics and building hands-on projects, rural teens experience significant growth in cultural understanding and computer science skills.

Community

Working with other rural teens from all across the world to embark on global Adventures, the teens of RCS cement a strong sense of community and teamwork.

Who Can Join?
