What is the Local Website Initiative (LWI)?

A program that helps build real-world coding experience to rural teens by building modern, affordable websites for local businesses that don't already have one.

Real Client Experience

Students work directly with small business owners, learning how to gather requirements, communicate professionally, and deliver polished products.

Hands-On Web Development

Rural teens work on real, fully functional websites that will be deployed into the real world.

Community Impact

In working with small business owners in need of affordable websites, teens do a true service to businesses in their community.

Fundraising

This program is a unique, domain-specific fundraiser to help RCS teens embark on computer science opportunities.

Who Can Join?

Rural Middle & High School Students

LWI is tailored for rural teenagers who want real-life coding experience through developing client projects.

Beginner & Advanced Developers

Developers, beginners and advanced alike, are warmly invited to work on LWI for the betterment of business in the rural community.

Local Businesses Without a Website

Local businesses that don't have a website already are whole-heartedly welcome to request an affordable yet modern website from us.
